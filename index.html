How it works

Four steps. Zero stress.

No WhatsApp chasing. No cash-in-hand ambiguity. No waking up the next day wondering if the job was actually done right.

STEP 1

Tell us what's broken

Post your job in two minutes. Add photos, describe the problem, say when you need someone. That's it from you for now.

STEP 2

Compare verified pros

Verified electricians and plumbers send you a call-out fee, a rough price, and when they can be there. Their ratings and track record sit right next to their quote.

STEP 3

The real price gets locked in

Your pro arrives, inspects the job, and sets the final price. That price is locked. We hold your money until you're happy. If you don't like the price, you walk away and only pay the call-out fee.

STEP 4

Your pro gets paid when the job is done

Approve the work and your pro gets paid. Not happy? Tell us within 7 days. Your money doesn't move until it's sorted.

Pricing

You only pay when the job is done

No subscriptions. No monthly fees. No upfront anything. Our fee comes out of the job, only once it's complete.

For homeowners
3.5% on the job total
One Spanna fee, shown up front before you approve anything. Covers safe payment handling, real support, and a cover fund if things go sideways.
  • Transparent to the rand before you commit
  • Includes a 7-day window to flag problems after the job
  • R50 booking fee on call-out, credited back on completion
  • Regular customers who use us often qualify for a reduced rate
Best value
For tradespeople
2.5% on completed jobs only
No lead fees. No subscriptions. Nothing until you've done the work and been paid. Compare that to what other platforms take.
  • Pay nothing until the job is done and money is in your account
  • Hit 20 completed jobs in a year and your rate drops to 2%
  • Fee applies to the total job value
  • Your call-out fee stays with you even if the customer declines the final quote
The cover fund
1% on every completed job
A small portion of each job goes into a ring-fenced fund. It pays for dispute resolution and covers any liability if something serious goes wrong.
  • Already included in the customer's 3.5% all-in fee
  • Ring-fenced — never touched for operating costs
  • Gives both sides real recourse when things go wrong
  • The fund that makes "Trades you can trust" more than a tagline
